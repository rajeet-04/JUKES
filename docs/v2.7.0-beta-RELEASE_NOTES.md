# v2.7.0-beta - RELEASE NOTES

Release Date: 2026-10-21

## Overview

v2.7.0-beta adds **Jam** (listen together with a code or link, no account) and **playlist share links**, and improves analytics.

## 🚀 What's new

- **Jam.** Start a listening room from the Home header and invite people with a 6-character code or a link (`https://j.rajeet.in/j/<code>`). No account needed.
  - Everyone hears the same song at the same time on their own phone, kept in sync automatically.
  - Everyone has equal controls and edits one shared queue. The host (longest-connected member) hands over automatically when they leave.
  - "Start with what I'm playing" seeds the room from your queue.
  - Play, Play next, Add to queue and Mix from Search, Library, Home and the player go to the room; the player, mini player and notification controls drive it too.
  - A host-run radio keeps the queue topped up; playing a new song or tapping Mix refreshes it.
  - Songs your phone has no Spotify id for are matched on Spotify so friends can play them; the next songs are prepared ahead of time.
  - The app rejoins your Jam when reopened or after a dropped connection.
  - Leaving a Jam keeps the songs that were coming up as your own queue.
- **Share a playlist by link.** Library > playlist menu > Share link creates `https://j.rajeet.in/p/<id>`; opening it in JUKES offers to save the playlist. No login needed.
- Analytics: one anonymous id per install, real value types, accurate event times, and new events for song completed/skipped, Jam, sharing, screens, playlists, queue edits and the sleep timer.

## 🔧 Changes

- Analytics now use the PostHog SDK's own offline queue instead of a hand-rolled one; numbers are sent as numbers and events carry the time they happened.

## 🐛 Fixes

- Home header: the Jam and Settings buttons no longer overlap.

## Notes

- Jam and share links run on the `j.rajeet.in` Cloudflare Worker; the Worker is already deployed.
- Jam follower and radio run while the app is open; swiping the app away pauses syncing until it is reopened.
