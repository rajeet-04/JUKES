# Unreleased Changes

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Share a playlist by link**: Library > playlist menu > Share link creates `https://j.rajeet.in/p/<id>`. Opening it in JUKES offers to save the playlist; no login needed. Links stay alive while they are used.
- **Jam**: start a listening room from the Home header and invite people with a 6-character code or a link (`https://j.rajeet.in/j/<code>`). No account needed.
  - Everyone hears the same song at the same time on their own phone, kept in sync automatically.
  - Everyone has equal controls, and the queue is shared and editable by all.
  - The host (the longest-connected member) hands over automatically when they leave.
  - "Start with what I'm playing" seeds the room from your current queue.
  - Play, Play next, Add to queue and Mix from Search, Library, Home and the player all go to the room; the player, mini player and notification controls drive it too.
  - A host-run radio keeps the queue topped up; playing a new song or tapping Mix refreshes it.
  - Songs your phone has no Spotify id for are matched on Spotify so friends can play them.
  - The next songs are prepared ahead of time, and the app rejoins your Jam when reopened or after a dropped connection.
- Long-press menu on every Home card and row (play, play next, add to queue).

### Fixed
- Home header: the Jam and Settings buttons no longer overlap.
