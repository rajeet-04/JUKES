# v2.5.0-beta - RELEASE NOTES

Release Date: 2026-10-12

## Overview

v2.5.0-beta reworks Up Next and recommendations around YouTube Music's own radio, turns the mini player and full player into one animated surface, adds a new vector app icon, and lets you save the queue as a playlist. It also fixes the queue repeating songs or filling with unrelated ones, and keeps favourites and play counts when a streamed song is downloaded.

## 🚀 Features & Enhancements

### Up Next and recommendations

- Up Next shows the whole radio at once. Songs not fetched yet are greyed and colour up as they become ready; tapping one fetches it right away and plays it.
- Only the next few songs load ahead (the Recommendation Queue setting, 5–15), and each joins the player as soon as it is ready. This also cuts load on the backend.
- Recommendations follow the playing song's YouTube Music radio in its own order, one radio at a time; songs heard or skipped lately wait at the end.
- Swipe right on a queued song to play a copy next. A waiting song is fetched first and held as "Plays next".
- Up Next rows are flat Library-style rows with the same glass swipe reveal.
- Save the queue as a playlist from Up Next or the player's ⋮ menu, optionally including the waiting songs.
- Bracketed text in titles is read, not cut: upload labels are ignored, versions (Remix, Live, Slowed...) are different songs, and credits are told apart by length.

### Look and feel

- The mini player grows into the full player and shrinks back (container transform with shared artwork), with staged content and gesture-driven settle.
- One spring motion language across screens and press feedback; smoother lyrics transitions.
- Neon edge on the selected Home / Search / Library tab, including the folded tab button beside the mini player.
- New fully vector app icon (J-note over a vinyl record) with a monochrome layer for themed icons.
- Playlists without a cover get art built from their songs (1, 2, 3 or a 2x2 mosaic), or a tinted tile when empty.
- The mini player stays visible on all Settings pages.

## 🔧 Fixes & Under the Hood

- Downloading a streamed song keeps its favourite, play count and last played.
- "Fetch from another source" now swaps in the new audio and restarts from 0:00.
- Recommended songs no longer repeat in blocks or get queued twice; reordering the queue or reopening the app no longer resets the recommendation session.
- A YouTube radio that doesn't start with its seed song is ignored instead of filling the queue with unrelated songs.
- Glass sheet headers (Up Next) are readable again.
- Library snackbars and the selection bar follow the player chrome as it folds.
- Downloading a song in its first seconds no longer restarts it; opening the app mid-queue no longer flashes another song; starting mixes or clearing the queue no longer blinks.
- Removed unused drawables and the old PNG/WebP launcher bitmaps.
