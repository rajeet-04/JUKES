# v3.1.1-stable - RELEASE NOTES

Release Date: 2026-11-16

Android only. The macOS, Windows and Linux apps stay on 3.1.0.

## Overview

v3.1.1-stable is a bug-fix release for the Android app: background playback, the notification and the lyric line under the artwork.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Resume from the notification.** Pausing in the background and pressing play in the notification could crash the app. It no longer does, and playback stays resumable for 10 minutes after a pause.
- **No sound with Battery Saver Playback.** Some phones (seen on iQOO/vivo) say they support audio offload but play nothing. JUKE now checks before offloading, and if an offloaded song still doesn't progress it switches back to normal output by itself.
- **Lyric line under the artwork.** The current line was missing on some screen sizes.
- **Blank notification artwork.** The media card in the notification shade and on the lock screen showed a placeholder instead of the artwork on vivo and iQOO phones.
- **Notification icons.** The favourite and download icons are the same size as previous/play/next.
- **Lyrics without timings.** Lyrics that arrive without timings in the synced field are shown as plain lyrics instead of nothing.
- **Romanized Urdu.** میں is written "main" or "mein" according to the line's Hindi.

## 🔧 Changes

- Media3, the playback library, is updated from 1.5.0 to 1.11.1.

## ⚠️ Notes

- The Android APK installs over 3.1.0-stable and keeps your library.
- Battery Saver Playback does nothing on phones where offload doesn't work; the setting still shows.
